Designer prototype privacy

19 November 2026

This prototype runs in your browser. It does not use an Impact Engines account, model proxy, analytics service or uninstall survey.

Your selected model provider

Your messages, relevant page content, attached files and requested screenshots are sent to the model provider you configure. Voice input, if enabled, goes to the selected voice provider. Their terms and data handling apply. Browser automation running locally does not mean hosted-model requests stay on your device.

Browser storage

API keys, conversations, attachments and settings are stored locally in the extension's browser storage. Keys go to their configured providers. Remove keys in Providers settings and delete conversations or all extension data in Data settings.

Browser access

The inherited browser engine requests debugger and broad browser permissions. This prototype is intended for Survey Solutions Designer, but its permission manifest is not yet restricted to Designer origins. Use a dedicated test questionnaire and inspect proposed changes.

Telemetry and attribution

Production telemetry is disabled in code, including when upstream analytics environment variables are set. Store-rating prompts and the upstream uninstall survey are disabled. This is an Impact Engines derivative of NanoBrowser; it is not a World Bank product or endorsement.
